I co-organized the [CSS Workshop: AI and Society](https://www.iris.uni-stuttgart.de/news/CSS-Workshop-AI-and-Society/) at the University of Stuttgart on February 25, 2026, bringing together researchers from IRIS, the IMS, and CSS in Stuttgart and the Chair of Data Science in the Economic and Social Sciences at the University of Mannheim to explore how computational approaches can support the study of social, cultural, and institutional questions about AI.
