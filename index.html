Esra Dönmez

IMS, University of Stuttgart | ILCC, University of Edinburgh | Interaction Lab, Heriot-Watt University

I am a final-year PhD Candidate at the IMS, University of Stuttgart, mentored by Agnieszka Faleńska. My research focuses on socially embedded AI systems, spanning responsible/ethical AI and sociotechnical approaches to model design, alignment operationalization and objective trade-offs, sociotechnical evaluation and benchmarking, and the societal implications of AI.

Currently, I am at the University of Edinburgh and Heriot-Watt University for a research stay working with Björn Ross and Gavin Abercrombie. Earlier this year, I spent three months at MilaNLP, mentored by Dirk Hovy. I am investigating value pluralism in AI alignment, with the goal of developing language technologies that represent diverse human perspectives and can dynamically adapt to new and/or minority perspectives.
